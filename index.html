About Me

Exploring urban-climate interactions through high-resolution modeling

Now at: Arizona State University (Postdoctoral Research Scholar) Also: University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Visiting & Collaborative Researcher)

I am a climate scientist focused on how urban growth reshapes local weather, extreme heat, and intense rainfall. I blend high-resolution WRF/UrbanWRF simulations with reanalysis and satellite data to unpack land–atmosphere feedbacks from neighborhood to megaregion scales.

I translate those diagnostics into decision-ready guidance for cities planning for heat waves, flooding, and compound hazards, working with collaborators across urban systems, hydrology, and public agencies.

  • Run convection-permitting experiments to isolate urbanization impacts on heat and precipitation.
  • Validate simulations with ERA5, AORC, and remote-sensing datasets for actionable skill.
  • Deliver clear stories and visuals that connect model insights to resilience decisions.
